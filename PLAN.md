# HS601 — Entrepreneurship — PLAN

**Semester:** 6 · **Credits:** 2 · **Level:** Advanced
**Theory:** 30 hrs · **Lab:** 15 hrs · **Tools:** Lean Canvas, pitch decks, financial models, market research

## Syllabus

### Unit 1 — Entrepreneurship Foundations (6 lectures)
1. What entrepreneurship is — and what it is not
2. Startup vs small business vs corporate innovation
3. The founder's journey — stages and milestones
4. Idea generation and opportunity recognition
5. Problem-first thinking — the customer's pain
6. Common founder myths and the reality

### Unit 2 — Customer Discovery and Validation (6 lectures)
1. Customer segments and personas
2. Problem interviews — asking the right questions
3. Solution interviews — testing the value proposition
4. The Lean Startup — build-measure-learn
5. Minimum Viable Product (MVP) design
6. Traction — what to measure and when

### Unit 3 — Business Models (6 lectures)
1. The Business Model Canvas
2. The Lean Canvas for startups
3. Revenue models — subscription, transaction, ads, licensing
4. Unit economics — CAC, LTV, payback
5. Pricing strategies for software products
6. Market sizing — TAM, SAM, SOM

### Unit 4 — Fundraising and Finance (6 lectures)
1. Bootstrapping vs raising money
2. Funding stages — pre-seed, seed, Series A and beyond
3. What investors look for — team, market, traction
4. Term sheets and valuation basics
5. Financial modelling for early-stage startups
6. Cap tables and equity distribution

### Unit 5 — Building and Scaling (6 lectures)
1. Founding team and co-founder fit
2. Early hires — roles and equity
3. Product-led growth
4. Sales and marketing for technical founders
5. Legal and compliance basics — incorporation, IP
6. Scaling challenges — culture, processes, cash

## Practical Labs (15)

1. Idea brainstorm — 20 problems you could solve
2. Pick one and write a problem statement
3. Conduct 5 customer discovery interviews
4. Write a persona and a lean canvas
5. Design your MVP — features vs anti-features
6. Build a landing page for validation
7. Run a smoke test — measure interest
8. Unit economics — calculate CAC and LTV
9. Price your product — three pricing hypotheses
10. Market sizing — TAM / SAM / SOM
11. Write a financial model for 24 months
12. Build a pitch deck — 10 slides
13. Practice the 3-minute pitch (recorded)
14. Cap table exercise — split equity among 3 founders
15. Final — pitch, business plan, and reflection

## Assignments

1. Customer discovery report — interviews and insights
2. Lean canvas for your chosen startup
3. Unit economics and financial model
4. Pitch deck and video pitch
5. Final reflection — what you would do differently
